## 1. Purpose

This AI Transparency Policy explains how CyberSec ("we," "us," "our") uses artificial intelligence within CyberSec Toolkit (the "Platform"), what it is used for, its limitations, and the role human oversight plays. We believe users are entitled to a clear understanding of when they are interacting with AI-generated content.

## 2. Where AI Is Used

The Platform currently uses AI in one feature: generating security reports that interpret and summarize the technical output of scanning tools, such as open ports, service banners, HTTP headers, and certificate details, into a more readable, actionable format.

AI is not currently used to make account, billing, or access-control decisions.

## 3. How AI-Generated Content Is Produced

When you request an AI-assisted report, the relevant Scan Data is sent to an AI provider along with a structured prompt designed to produce a consistent, security-relevant report. The response is returned to you through the Platform. We do not manually review every AI-generated report before it is shown to you.

## 4. AI Providers

AI processing on the Platform is currently performed using Groq and Google Gemini. We may add, remove, or change AI providers over time as the Platform evolves; this Policy will be updated to reflect material changes.

## 5. Limitations of AI-Generated Content

AI-generated content is produced by automated language models and has inherent limitations, including that it:

- May be incomplete or omit relevant findings
- May misinterpret technical data or draw an incorrect conclusion
- May be phrased with more confidence than the underlying analysis supports
- Reflects the state of the underlying model at the time the report was generated, and may not reflect the most current threat intelligence

AI-generated reports are a starting point for understanding your scan results, not a substitute for professional security review.

## 6. Human Oversight

We monitor the quality of AI-generated output on an ongoing basis and adjust our prompting and processing approach as issues are identified. We encourage users to apply their own judgment, and where appropriate the judgment of a qualified security professional, before acting on AI-generated recommendations.

## 7. Use of Your Data with AI Providers

Scan Data submitted for AI-assisted report generation is sent to our AI providers solely to generate your report. We do not use your Scan Data to train our own AI models. Whether an AI provider uses submitted data to train its own underlying models is governed by that provider's own terms and data handling practices; we select providers with reference to their stated data handling commitments, but we do not control their internal training practices directly.

## 8. No Automated Significant Decisions

We do not use AI to make automated decisions that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects concerning you, such as decisions about legal status, employment, or creditworthiness. AI on the Platform is limited to interpreting technical scan output for informational purposes.

## 9. Identifying AI-Generated Content

Reports and content produced by the Platform's AI features are labeled as AI-generated within the product interface, so that you can distinguish them from raw scan output.

## 10. Providing Feedback

If you believe an AI-generated report is inaccurate, misleading, or otherwise problematic, please let us know using the contact details below. Feedback helps us improve the quality of this feature over time.

## 11. Policy Review

This Policy is reviewed periodically and updated as our use of AI, our AI providers, or applicable law evolves.
