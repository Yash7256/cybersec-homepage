## 1. Purpose

This Security Policy sets out the security principles and controls that govern how CyberSec ("we," "us," "our") builds, operates, and maintains CyberSec Toolkit (the "Platform"). It is intended to give customers, security teams, and other stakeholders a clear, honest description of our security program.

This Policy is a formal statement of our internal practices. A plain-language summary intended for a general audience is available in our Security Overview.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to the CyberSec Toolkit application, its supporting infrastructure, and the data processed through it. It applies to CyberSec personnel and to sub-processors engaged to support the Platform, to the extent described in Section 8.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

Security responsibilities are currently held centrally, given the size of our team. Responsibilities include:

- Setting and maintaining this Policy and related security documentation
- Reviewing access to production systems and data
- Triaging and responding to vulnerability reports and security incidents
- Evaluating the security posture of third-party sub-processors before they are engaged

As our team grows, we intend to formalize distinct security roles and a documented approval chain for security-relevant decisions.

## 4. Access Control

Access to production systems, the production database, and customer data is restricted to personnel who require it to operate or maintain the Platform. We apply the following principles:

- Authentication is required before any access to internal systems or the production environment
- Access is granted based on least privilege and reviewed periodically
- Customer-facing authentication is managed through a dedicated third-party authentication provider, with support for multi-factor authentication where offered
- Credentials and secrets used by the Platform are not stored in plaintext in source control

## 5. Data Classification

We classify data handled by the Platform into the following broad categories, each of which is subject to controls proportional to its sensitivity:

- **Account data** — information needed to identify and authenticate a user, such as name and email address
- **Scan Data** — technical output generated by Platform tools when a user submits a scan target
- **Operational data** — logs, metrics, and diagnostic information generated by the Platform itself
- **AI-generated content** — reports and analyses produced by AI processing of Scan Data

Handling requirements for each category, including retention, are further described in our Data Retention Policy.

## 6. Secure Development

Changes to the Platform follow a development process intended to reduce the introduction of security defects, including peer review of code changes, dependency review, pre-release testing, and post-deployment validation. Further detail is available in our Security Overview.

## 7. Vulnerability Management

We maintain an ongoing process to identify and remediate vulnerabilities affecting the Platform, including internal review, dependency monitoring, and external reports received under our Responsible Disclosure Policy. Identified issues are prioritized based on severity and exploitability, and remediation is tracked through to verification.

## 8. Third-Party and Sub-Processor Risk Management

Before engaging a third-party sub-processor to support the Platform, we consider the provider's relevant security practices and the sensitivity of the data that would be shared with them. Our current sub-processors are disclosed in our Privacy Policy. We review this list periodically and update our Privacy Policy when it changes materially.

## 9. Incident Response

In the event of a suspected security incident affecting the Platform or customer data, we follow a structured process covering preparation, identification, containment, investigation, recovery, and post-incident review, as described in our Security Overview. Where an incident involves personal data and triggers a notification obligation under applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we will notify affected users and, where required, the Data Protection Board of India or other applicable authority, within the timeframes required by law.

## 10. Logging and Monitoring

We maintain logging of significant account, application, and infrastructure events to support both operational reliability and security investigations. Logs are retained in accordance with our Data Retention Policy and access to them is restricted to authorized personnel.

## 11. Business Continuity

We maintain backups of critical data and apply infrastructure practices intended to support recovery of the Platform in the event of a significant disruption. As the Platform and our team grow, we intend to formalize a documented business continuity and disaster recovery plan with defined recovery objectives.

## 12. Personnel Security

Personnel with access to production systems or customer data are expected to follow this Policy and related security practices, including safeguarding credentials, using access only for legitimate purposes, and reporting suspected security issues promptly.

## 13. Policy Review

This Policy is reviewed periodically and updated as our practices, infrastructure, or applicable law evolve. Material changes will be reflected in the "Effective Date / Last Updated" field at the top of this page.

## 14. Enforcement

Personnel or sub-processors found to be in violation of this Policy may be subject to corrective action, up to and including termination of access or of the engagement, as applicable.
