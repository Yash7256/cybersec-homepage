## 1. Purpose

This Data Retention Policy explains how long CyberSec ("we," "us," "our") retains different categories of data collected through CyberSec Toolkit (the "Platform"), and how that data is deleted once it is no longer needed. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to Personal Information, Scan Data, and operational data processed through the Platform, as described in our Privacy Policy.

## 3. Retention Principles

We apply the following principles when determining how long to retain data:

- We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose it was collected for
- We consider our legal and regulatory obligations, including obligations under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, when setting retention periods
- We aim to delete or anonymize data once it is no longer needed for an active purpose, subject to Section 7

## 4. Retention Periods by Data Category

| Data Category | Typical Retention Period | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Account information (name, email, organization) | For the life of the account, plus a limited period after closure | Retained to support account recovery and legal obligations after closure |
| Authentication data | For the life of the account | Managed through our authentication provider |
| Scan Data and scan history | For the life of the account, or a defined rolling window | May be deleted or archived after a period of inactivity |
| AI-generated reports | Linked to the retention of the underlying Scan Data | Deleted alongside the Scan Data they were generated from |
| Technical and usage logs | A limited rolling period | Retained primarily for security monitoring and troubleshooting |
| Support communications | For a limited period after resolution | Retained to support quality review and follow-up |
| Billing records (once applicable) | As required by applicable tax and financial recordkeeping law | Retained beyond account closure where legally required |

Specific retention windows for each category are set operationally and may be updated as the Platform evolves. This table reflects our general approach rather than fixed day-count values that would require frequent revision of this Policy.

## 5. Backups

Backups of the production database may retain data for a limited period after it has been deleted from the live system, in order to support disaster recovery. Data contained in backups is subject to the same protections described in our Security Policy and is not used for any purpose other than restoration.

## 6. Account Closure and Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your account and associated Personal Information at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 10, or, where available, through account settings within the Platform. We will process valid deletion requests within the timeframe required by applicable law, subject to the exceptions described in Section 7.

## 7. Legal Holds

We may retain data beyond the periods described above where necessary to comply with a legal obligation, resolve a dispute, enforce our agreements, or respond to a valid request from a court, regulator, or the Data Protection Board of India.

## 8. Secure Deletion

When data is deleted in accordance with this Policy, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is removed from active production systems. Residual copies may persist in backups for a limited period, as described in Section 5, until those backups are themselves rotated out in the ordinary course.

## 9. Policy Review

This Policy is reviewed periodically and updated as our data handling practices, infrastructure, or applicable law evolve.
