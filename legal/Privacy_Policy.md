## 1. Introduction

CyberSec ("we," "us," "our," or "the Company") operates CyberSec Toolkit, an AI-assisted network security intelligence platform (the "Platform" or "Service"), accessible at cybersec1.tech. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what rights you have with respect to that information.

This Policy is drafted with reference to the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made thereunder, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDPA") of India, as these govern our processing of personal data as a Data Fiduciary. Where our users are located outside India, we have also had regard to internationally recognized privacy principles, including those under the GDPR and CCPA, without representing that we are otherwise subject to those frameworks.

By creating an account or otherwise using the Platform, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, you should not use the Platform.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to all visitors, registered users, and organizations that access CyberSec Toolkit through our website, dashboard, or associated interfaces. It covers information collected through:

- Account registration and authentication
- Use of the Platform's security tools and features
- Automated technical data collected during normal operation of the Platform
- Communications between you and us

This Policy does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that may be linked from the Platform. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of any third party you interact with separately from CyberSec Toolkit.

## 3. Definitions

- **"Personal Information" / "Personal Data"** means any information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with an identified or identifiable individual (referred to as a "Data Principal" under the DPDPA).
- **"Data Fiduciary"** means the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data. CyberSec acts as the Data Fiduciary in respect of personal data processed through the Platform.
- **"Scan Data"** means the results, metadata, and technical output generated when you use a Platform tool against a domain, IP address, or other target you submit.
- **"User," "you," "your"** means an individual or organization that accesses or uses the Platform.
- **"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Information, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.
- **"Service Provider" / "Data Processor"** means a third party that processes information on our behalf under our instructions.

## 10. AI Processing

CyberSec Toolkit uses artificial intelligence, provided through Groq and Google Gemini, to help interpret technical Scan Data and generate human-readable security reports.

- **What AI processes:** AI processing is applied to Scan Data (such as open ports, service banners, headers, and certificate information) and, where relevant, to your prompts or questions submitted to AI-assisted features.
- **Why:** AI processing converts raw technical findings into contextualized, actionable security guidance.
- **Limitations:** AI-generated output is produced using automated language models and may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. It is provided for informational purposes and should not be treated as a substitute for professional security judgment.
- **Human verification:** We encourage you to independently verify any AI-generated finding before relying on it for security or business decisions.
- **No automated significant decisions:** We do not use AI to make automated decisions that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects concerning you, including decisions related to legal status, employment, or credit.
